# Road safety analysis

## Overview
The analysis focuses on two research questions:

- Do crashes occurring on Country roads result in a higher number of injuries or fatalities than crashes on Metro roads?
- Is crash severity associated with whether a crash occurs during the day or at night?


## Skills Used
- Data cleaning
- Statistical analysis
- Data visualisation
